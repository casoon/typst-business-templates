The package ships 21 templates plus a diagram workflow. This catalogue shows what each one produces
and how it is solved. Every picture is the first page of a real example from `examples/`, compiled
with typstgen; the PDF opens from the caption. The
[reference](../reference/document-types/) lists the functions, calling conventions and schemas.

## Three ways to build a document

| Kind | Content comes as | Layout | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Accounting documents** | a JSON file, validated by a schema | DIN 5008 letter with address window and four-column company footer | [Accounting documents](./accounting/) |
| **Documents** | Typst text after a `#show:` rule, metadata as parameters | title page, optional table of contents, branded footer | [Documents with a title page](./documents/) |
| **Handouts, publications, diagrams** | Typst text with helper functions, or a chartlet spec | free layout, components, SVG | [Handouts, publications and diagrams](./handouts-and-diagrams/) |

Accounting documents and documents read the same `company.json` (name, address, bank, logo,
colours), so changing the company data changes every one of them.

## Which documents work well together

These are suggestions, not features of the package: the templates are independent, but they share
`company.json` and the same data shapes, so they chain naturally.

- **Sales chain:** quotation request, then offer, order confirmation, delivery note, invoice.
  Offer, order confirmation and invoice take positions in `items` of the same shape, so the
  positions can be copied forward and only `metadata` changes.
- **Dunning:** invoice, reminder (levels 1 to 3), credit note for corrections. The reminder lists
  `outstanding_invoices` by number, so it needs no copy of the positions.
- **Project chain:** proposal, concept, specification, contract, SLA, minutes during the project,
  documentation at the end. They share title page and branding, so they read as one set.
- **Billing from time:** the time sheet's hours total is what you put into an invoice position; the
  sheet is the attachment the client signs.
